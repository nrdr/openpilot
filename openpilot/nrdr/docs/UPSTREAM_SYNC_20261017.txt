What changes on Honda

- Keep the Civic PCM sent-command model and 10 Hz transmit-horizon correction.
  Add MVL's positive-acceleration speed-lead floor and alpha-learning guard.
  The Civic gates use the planner request, not its separate PID correction.
  Other Nidec PCM platforms receive the upstream speed-lead guard too.
- Interceptor behavior DOES change: integral gain is now 0.3, and the pedal
  multiplier is 0.35/0.4/0.6/1.0 at 0/3/6/10 m/s. These are MVL's measured
  ILX-based changes, not proof of improvement on every Honda. NRDR's explicit
  Roen multiplier override remains 1.0. No baseline-parity claim is made.
- Integra uses ABS movement checks when ENGINE_DATA is absent. Matching safety
  RX checks are included, with no change to torque or acceleration TX limits.
- Include Bosch HUD simplification and 2027 CR-V Hybrid support.
- Register MVL's 21 new lateral gain/shape keys. They have no active consumer
  in this controller yet; this does not override NRDR firmware calibrations.
